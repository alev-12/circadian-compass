# Circadian Compass

Free, private, in-browser sleep tools. No accounts, no tracking, no backend: everything runs in your browser and anything saved stays on your device.

- Live circadian rhythm curve with your current body-clock phase
- Bedtime / wake-time calculator built on 90-minute sleep cycles, with a wind-down timeline
- Chronotype quiz (Lion / Bear / Wolf / Dolphin) with a light-exposure plan
- Sleep debt tracker with a 30-day trend
- Jet lag and shift-work adjuster
- Nap timer and caffeine cutoff calculator
- Wind-down checklist

General sleep-science guidance only, not medical advice.
